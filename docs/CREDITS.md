# Image credits

The photographs in the README screenshots are not mine. Both are **CC0** —
public domain dedication, no attribution required — which is why they were
chosen: a screenshot of an edited photo is arguably a derivative of it, and a
share-alike licence on one would sit awkwardly inside an MIT repository.

| Shown in | Photograph | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `screenshot.png`, `feature-looks.png`, `feature-crop.png` | [Sunset Over The Grand Canyon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sunset_Over_The_Grand_Canyon_(Unsplash).jpg) (Unsplash, via Wikimedia Commons) | CC0 |
| `feature-subject.png` | [Sitting In the Desert](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sitting_In_the_Desert_(Unsplash).jpg) (Unsplash, via Wikimedia Commons) | CC0 |

Credited here anyway, because being told where a picture came from is worth
more than the licence strictly requires.

The screenshots are captured by driving the real app in headless Chrome against
the dev server — WebGL2 runs under SwiftShader, so what they show is the actual
render graph rather than a mock-up.
